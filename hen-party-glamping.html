Whole-site hire · Private meadow

Our bell tent retreat sits in a private meadow on our family's organic farm. When you book, the whole site is yours: the tents, the hot tub, the fire pit and the long table under the stretch tent. No other guests, just your group.

A hen do your way

Your own field for the weekend

Picture long evenings round the fire, dinner together under the stretch tent and a soak in the wood-fired hot tub, with nobody else on site. The five bell tents each have a proper king-size bed with bedding, so ten of you sleep in real comfort.

There's room for up to 20 in total if you bring air beds or extra tents. The more the merrier. I'm always happy to help with the planning, from local suppliers to getting everyone here.

A quick note on the photos: we've since moved the bell tents to a different field on the farm, a little further from the river to keep everyone safe. The new field is just as beautiful, quiet and private.

Common questions

Hen do questions

Is the site private for a hen party?+

Yes. Every booking is for the whole site, so the meadow, bell tents, stretch tent, hot tub and fire pit are just for your group. No other guests.

How many people can come on a hen do?+

The five bell tents sleep 10 in king-size beds with bedding provided. There's room for up to 20 in total if you bring air beds or extra tents, so the more the merrier. Just let me know your numbers when you book.

What is there to do for a hen party in Worcestershire?+

On site there's the hot tub, the fire pit, a long table under the stretch tent and plenty of room for games. The Talbot pub is a five-minute walk away, and I'm happy to point you towards local suppliers for grazing boards, cakes and activities.

Ready to plan the hen do?

Send me a message and we'll get your dates sorted. I know the area well and I'm happy to help with extras, local suppliers and getting everyone here.
