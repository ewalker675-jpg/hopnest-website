Two huts, one view

Wide views and proper dark skies

Both huts sit side by side on a small rise, looking out across the Teme Valley. You get big open views, lovely sunsets and, on a clear night, more stars than you'll have seen in a long time. Neither hut catches the early morning sun, so a lie-in is very much on the cards.

They're within sight of each other but each feels private, with its own fire pit and net bed. Book one for a couple's break, or take both if you're travelling with friends or family and want your own space.

A bit of time out

Slow down for a few days

Light the fire, open a bottle, lie out on the net bed and watch the stars come out. It might sound a bit mad when life has so many stresses, but a few quiet days in the countryside can settle you more than you'd think.

Most people leave feeling rested, grounded and content. That's what we hope the huts give you.

Common questions

Questions about the shepherd huts

How many people does each shepherd hut sleep?+

Each hut sleeps two in a king-size bed, with bedding and towels provided. If there are four of you, you can book both huts together.

Can we book both huts together?+

Yes. Skylark and Nuthatch sit within sight of each other, so booking both works really well for two couples, friends or family travelling together, while everyone still has their own private space.

What's inside the huts?+

Each hut has a king-size bed, a full kitchen with a fridge, an en suite shower and toilet, mains electric and an electric heater. Outside there's a fire pit with chairs and a net bed for stargazing.

Are the huts warm enough outside summer?+

Yes. Both huts have mains electric and an electric heater, so they stay cosy on cooler evenings.

Can we bring our dog?+

Please ask before booking and I'll let you know what works for your dates.

Where are the shepherd huts?+

They're on a small hill on our family's organic farm near Martley in Worcestershire, about 25 minutes from Worcester and 50 minutes from Birmingham. I'll send the exact address and directions when you book.

Coming with a bigger group?

Our bell tent retreat sleeps 10 in king-size beds and up to 20 with extra tents, with a hot tub, outdoor kitchen and the whole meadow to yourselves.
