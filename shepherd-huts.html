The hut

Everything you need for a proper break

Inside there's a king-size bed made up for you, towels, a little kitchen with a fridge, and your own shower and loo. There's electric and a heater too, so it's lovely and cosy even when it's chilly out.

Questions

Questions about the hut

How many does the hut sleep?+

Two, in a king-size bed. Bedding and towels are all there for you.

What's in the hut?+

A king-size bed, a little kitchen with a fridge, an en suite shower and loo, electric and a heater. Outside there's a fire pit with chairs and a net bed for stargazing.

Is it warm enough when it's cold out?+

Yes, there's electric and a heater, so it stays nice and cosy on chilly evenings.

Can I bring my dog?+

Just ask me before you book and I'll let you know.

Where is it?+

On our family's organic farm near Martley in Worcestershire, about 25 minutes from Worcester and 50 minutes from Birmingham. I'll send the exact address and directions when you book.

Coming with more people?

Have a look at our bell tent retreat. It sleeps 10 in proper beds and up to 20 with extra tents, and you get the hot tub and the whole field to yourselves.
