The Shepherd Hut · Near Martley, Worcestershire

A shepherd hut in Worcestershire

A cosy little hut for two on our organic farm in the Teme Valley. Stay for one night or a few.

Check availability

Sit back with a glass of wine while the sun sets across the valley. The hut is tucked away on our organic farm in the Teme Valley, just 20 minutes from Worcester, and it really does feel like a world of your own.

Inside there's a big comfy king-size bed, your own shower and loo, and a little kitchen so you can cook for yourselves. Outside there's a fire pit with the firewood ready for you, and a spot for two to sit and watch the evening go by. The Worcestershire Way walking route runs nearby too, if you fancy a good walk.

Questions

Questions about the hut

How many does the hut sleep?+

Two, in a king-size bed. Bedding and towels are all provided.

Is there a shower and loo?+

Yes, both are inside the hut, so no trekking across a field in the night.

Can I cook in the hut?+

Yes. There are gas hobs, an air fryer, a fridge, a kettle and a toaster, and cutlery is provided.

What's the minimum stay?+

Just one night, so it's perfect for a quick getaway.

What time is check-in and check-out?+

Check-in is from 3pm and check-out is by 11am.

Can I bring my dog?+

Sorry, dogs aren't allowed in the hut.

Where is it?+

On our organic farm in the Teme Valley near Martley, Worcestershire, just 20 minutes from Worcester. I'll send you the exact address and directions when you book.

Coming with more people?

Have a look at our bell tent retreat. It sleeps 10 in proper beds and up to 20 with extra tents, and you get the whole field to yourselves.
