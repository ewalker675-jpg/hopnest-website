Private Group Camping in Worcestershire

A field just for your group

We started hiring out this field because friends kept asking where they could camp together without being split across pitches or sharing with strangers. So we made it simple: one booking, one group, the whole field.

It's 1.5 acres of mown meadow edged by trees, on a working organic farm. People book it for reunions, big birthdays, family weekends, festival warm-ups and stag dos. You pitch where you like, cook how you like, and sit round the fire as long as you like.

Common questions

Questions about hiring the campsite

Where can I camp with a group of friends in England? +

Hopnest is a private campsite near Worcester that you hire as a whole group. Up to 20 people get sole use of a 1.5-acre field, with a hot outdoor shower, two portaloos, dry storage and a stone fire pit. No other campers are booked while you're here.

Can you hire a whole campsite for a group? +

Yes. Every booking is for the whole site, so you won't share the field, loos, shower or fire pit with anyone else. Pitch your tents wherever you like.

Can we book it for a stag do or birthday? +

Yes. We welcome stag dos, birthdays and reunions. Small speakers are fine; please keep the volume down after midnight for the livestock and neighbours. If you'd prefer tents already set up, see our bell tent retreat.

How far is it from Worcester? +

We're near Martley, about 10 miles west of Worcester, which is around 20 to 25 minutes by car. Birmingham is about 50 minutes via the M5. The nearest stations are Worcester Foregate Street and Shrub Hill, then a taxi.

Is there space for a big group? +

Yes, up to 20 people across the full 1.5 acres, so nobody is squeezed onto a small pitch. You'll have two portaloos, a hot gas shower in a cabin, a large stone fire pit with starter firewood, and a shipping container to keep food and kit dry.

What do we need to bring? +

There's no mains electricity, so bring tents, sleeping bags, a camping stove, cooking kit, food and drink. We provide the field, two portaloos, a hot shower, dry storage, the fire pit and starter firewood, with extra logs to buy on site.

Getting here

Near Martley, Worcestershire. About 25 minutes from Worcester and 50 from Birmingham. We'll send the exact address and directions when you book. The farm track isn't suitable for very low cars.
